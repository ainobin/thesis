# Detection of Abnormal Symptoms Using Acoustic-Spectrogram-Based Deep Learning (Kim et al., 2025)

This paper compares three spectrogram-based feature representations (raw spectrogram, Mel-spectrogram, MFCC) as inputs to a custom ResNet with bottleneck residual blocks for acoustic anomaly detection, finding that Mel-spectrogram features consistently yield the highest classification accuracy — directly validating the feature representation choice in your brick grading system.

---

## 1. Problem & Application Domain

- **Objective:** Detect abnormal health symptoms in livestock (pigs) by analyzing their vocalizations — a non-invasive, automated acoustic health monitoring system.
- **Why Acoustics:** Veterinary auscultation is labor-intensive and subjective; automated acoustic analysis offers scalable, objective, continuous monitoring.
- **Dataset:** Livestock vocalization recordings collected from operational farm environments, capturing both normal and abnormal (sick) animal sounds.

---

## 2. Feature Representation Comparison

The paper systematically compares three time-frequency feature representations derived from raw audio:

| Feature Type | Accuracy |
|--------------|----------|
| **Mel-Spectrogram** | **97.13%** |
| Raw Spectrogram (STFT) | 95.22% |
| MFCC | 93.78% |

- **Mel-Spectrogram outperforms** both raw STFT spectrograms and MFCCs. The Mel-filter bank's non-linear frequency scaling emphasizes perceptually relevant frequency ranges, making it the most discriminative representation for CNN-based acoustic classification.
- **Raw Spectrogram** carries all frequency information but includes regions that are irrelevant or noisy, potentially confusing the classifier.
- **MFCCs** compress the spectral information too aggressively, discarding details that the CNN could otherwise exploit for fine-grained discrimination.

---

## 3. Model Architecture: Custom ResNet

- **Bottleneck Residual Blocks:** The core building block uses a 1×1 → 3×3 → 1×1 convolution pattern that reduces then expands the channel dimension, improving computational efficiency and training stability in deeper networks.
- **Skip Connections:** Residual connections mitigate vanishing gradients, enabling effective training of deeper architectures on acoustic data.
- **Training:** The model was trained end-to-end on spectrogram inputs, learning hierarchical features from raw time-frequency representations.

---

## 4. Benchmark Comparisons

The proposed ResNet model was benchmarked against three baselines on Mel-spectrogram input:

| Model | Accuracy | F1-Score |
|-------|----------|----------|
| SVM (baseline) | Lower | Lower |
| Plain CNN (no residuals) | Lower | Lower |
| ResNet50 | Competitive | Competitive |
| **Proposed Custom ResNet** | **97.37%** | **96.87%** |

The custom ResNet with bottleneck blocks exceeded even ResNet50, demonstrating that a tailored architecture fine-tuned for the specific acoustic task outperforms generic off-the-shelf deep networks.
